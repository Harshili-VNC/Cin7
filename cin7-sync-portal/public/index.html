Privacy Policy

How VNC Global Business Edge collects, secures, encrypts, and processes your Cin7 Core enterprise financial data.

VNC Global Privacy Standard

Enterprise Data Protection Statement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Version 2.0.0

1. Information We Collect & Process

Cin7 Sync Platform functions as an automated data processing bridge between your Cin7 Core ERP (formerly DEAR Systems) and your Controller Financial Models (Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel). In executing automated daily synchronization, we process:

  • Cin7 Core Operational Data: Sales transaction ledgers, customer pricing rules, stock on-hand balances, purchase orders, product master catalogues, and landed unit costs.
  • Authentication Credentials: Cin7 Core API Account IDs, API Application Keys, and Google OAuth 2.0 refresh tokens.
  • Organizational Metadata: User full names, corporate email addresses, role permissions (Admin, Manager, Viewer), and audit logs.

2. Tenant Isolation & Zero Cross-Contamination

Every client organization is assigned an isolated cryptographic tenant workspace. Database records, API sync runs, immutable snapshot archives, and spreadsheet models are partitioned with strict Row-Level Security (RLS). Under no circumstances can data from one client organization be accessed by or leaked to another.

3. Cryptographic Security & Encryption

All API keys, secrets, and integration credentials stored within the platform are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with per-tenant salt vectors. Data in transit across Cin7 Core APIs, Google Cloud Bridges, and end-user browsers is encrypted exclusively over TLS 1.3.

4. Third-Party API Integrations

We only request minimum necessary scopes required to write normalized actuals to your spreadsheet models:

  • Google Sheets API: We only access and update the specific Controller Financial Spreadsheet authorized during onboarding. We never scan your broader Google Drive.
  • Cin7 Core REST API: We perform read-only extraction of actuals and never modify your primary accounting or inventory records in Cin7.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You maintain complete ownership of all synchronized financial records. Upon termination of service or upon written request to privacy@vnc.global, all stored credentials, snapshot caches, and sync archives are permanently purged within 30 days.

Questions regarding data privacy?

Reach out directly to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@vnc.global

VNC Global guarantees a monthly Service Level Availability of 99.9% for scheduled daily automated synchronizations, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 48 hours in advance.
